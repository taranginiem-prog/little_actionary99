Frequently asked questions

How long does shipping take?

Tracked delivery takes 3–5 days. Orders over $60 ship free.

What if my figure arrives damaged?

Every figure is mint-in-box guaranteed. Contact us and we'll send a replacement.

Can I return a figure?

Yes. Unopened figures can be returned within 30 days.

How big are the figures?

Each figure is about 5 inches tall with poseable arms and a display stand.

Are the characters original?

Yes. All Actionary figures are original cute designs made in-house.
