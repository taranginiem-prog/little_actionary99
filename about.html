Our story

Born from a doodle notebook

Actionary started with a notebook full of doodles: bunny girls, star fairies and tiny witches with big eyes. We wanted to turn them into figures you could hold, pose and display.

Today every character is an original design with a sweet face, a pastel outfit and poseable arms. No licensed characters, just our own cute world that keeps growing.
